# Mini CRM – Customer Management System

A web-based **Customer Relationship Management (CRM)** application designed to simplify customer data management, lead tracking, and sales activities through a centralized and user-friendly dashboard.

## Overview

The Mini CRM project aims to provide small businesses and individuals with an organized platform to manage customer information, monitor potential leads, and maintain customer relationships.

It demonstrates the fundamental concepts of full-stack web development, database management, CRUD operations, API integration, and user interface design.

## Key Features

- **Dashboard:** View customer statistics, lead summaries, and business activity.
- **Customer Management:** Add, view, update, and delete customer records.
- **Lead Management:** Track potential customers and their progress.
- **Search and Filtering:** Quickly find customers using names, contact details, or lead status.
- **Status Tracking:** Organize leads based on their current stages.
- **Database Integration:** Store and retrieve customer information.
- **User Authentication:** Provide secure access to the application.
- **Responsive Interface:** Access the application across different screen sizes.
